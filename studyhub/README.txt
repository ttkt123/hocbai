1. Mo index.html bang Chrome/Edge de hoc ngay, khong can cai dat.
2. De dua len GitHub Pages: upload index.html vao thu muc goc repository, bat Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main /root.
3. Cau hoi da duoc nhung san trong index.html; khong can server hay file questions.json de chay.
4. Luu tien do bang localStorage: dung cung trinh duyet/thiet bi. Xoa du lieu trinh duyet se mat lich su.
5. File questions.json la ban du lieu tach rieng de sua ve sau.
6. Che do hoc: chi chuyen cau khi chon dung. Che do thi: 30 cau ngau nhien, cho phep doi cau, chi cham khi nop.
7. Dap an goc trong 4 PDF deu la A. Web tron ngau nhien 4 lua chon va cap nhat vi tri dap an dung.
